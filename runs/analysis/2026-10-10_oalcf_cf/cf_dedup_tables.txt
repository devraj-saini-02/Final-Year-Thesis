CF DEDUPLICATION: CLUSTERS, ERRORS AND SAVINGS BY DISTANCE THRESHOLD
==============================================================================
Method: each session's corrective-feedback (CF) clips are clustered on their ECAPA embeddings
(cosine distance, average linkage, distance threshold t). The expert labels ONE clip per cluster
(the one the model is most confident is target) and that label is copied to the other members.
Lower t = tighter clusters = fewer copies, fewer errors.

Offline data: 6 OAL-CF baseline runs, stream-order seed 1 (ac/ha ac/yo cr/ky sea/vi sa/ml sa/skr),
113 sessions with CF, 6,726 CF clips = 5,665 true detections + 1,061 false alarms.
Measured on the baseline trajectories (tools/analysis/cf_dedup_offline.py).
