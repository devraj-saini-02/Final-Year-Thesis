4. READING
- Saves ~30% of the expert's corrective-feedback labels, 29.7-30.2% on every seed.
- Significantly better on both IMLM equations that count expert labels (18/20 languages, p = 0.002).
- No significant change on any accuracy metric (Eq.1, released repo scorer, DCF, FNR, FPR).
- Weak spots: sea/yue (worse on 3 of 5 seeds; early and mixed-group wrong copies) and ac/yo
  (one catastrophic run, seed 1). Plan and pre-declared rule: runs/reports/cfdedup__PLAN.md.
