Per-session clusters of the diversity extension: same tree as utils/diverse_query.py (cosine, average linkage), cut at several thresholds.
Means over sessions. Descriptive only (uses test-stream language labels).  mini cluster = fewer than 8 clips.

  homogeneity: 1.0 = every cluster holds one language | completeness: 1.0 = every language sits in one cluster | purity: share of clips whose cluster is mostly their own language
  pure clips: share of clips in a cluster that is >= 90% one language
  q med: median quota over clusters | q clip: quota of the cluster a typical CLIP sits in (median over clips), which is the quota the penalty actually meets, since queries land in the big clusters
  g=...: what a cluster's SECOND pick is multiplied by at that gamma, gamma**(1/q clip), with budget 8. Near 0 means the penalty has become a hard one-pick-per-cluster rule.
